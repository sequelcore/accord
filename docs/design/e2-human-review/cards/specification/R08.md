# R08

Review a partial authorization decision. Each Boolean says whether there is
at least one satisfied forbid, satisfied permit, residual permit, or residual
forbid. Residual means the policy still depends on unknown information. All 16
Boolean combinations are admitted. A satisfied forbid always denies. With no
satisfied or residual permit, deny even if a residual forbid exists. Otherwise
a residual forbid leaves the answer Unknown. Without forbids, a satisfied
permit allows; a residual permit alone leaves Unknown. Unknown grants no access
and is an authorization result, not an undecided solver outcome. Evaluation
and assignment of policies to these collections are outside this abstraction.

Find requirement violations or missing promises. Give a concrete input and
required result, or an excluded-input witness. Say which claim is affected.
Choose accept, request_change, or insufficient_information. If an artifact
cannot reveal a defect, say not_observable. After the task, rate confidence
0–100. Time limit: eight minutes. No tools, source browsing, or other packets.

```accord
enum PartialDecision { Deny, Allow, Unknown }

fn partial_decision(satisfied_forbid: Bool, satisfied_permit: Bool, residual_permit: Bool, residual_forbid: Bool) -> PartialDecision
    intent "Deny on a satisfied forbid or no possible permit; allow on a satisfied permit with no possible forbid; otherwise keep the decision unknown."
    ensures result == Deny implies satisfied_forbid or (not satisfied_permit and not residual_permit)
    ensures satisfied_forbid or (not satisfied_permit and not residual_permit) implies result == Deny
    ensures result == Allow implies not satisfied_forbid and satisfied_permit and not residual_forbid
    ensures not satisfied_forbid and satisfied_permit and not residual_forbid implies result == Allow
    ensures result == Unknown implies not satisfied_forbid and (satisfied_permit or residual_permit) and (residual_forbid or not satisfied_permit)
    ensures not satisfied_forbid and (satisfied_permit or residual_permit) and (residual_forbid or not satisfied_permit) implies result == Unknown
```
