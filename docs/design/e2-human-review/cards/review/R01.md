NoProgress for an already-sent failure. TimedOut, Cancelled, and NotStarted must
give Operator. All enum outcomes and all texts/lists are admitted. The result
is advisory; this pure function does not itself retry, stop, or contact anyone.

required result, or an excluded-input witness. Say which claim is affected.
Choose accept, request_change, or insufficient_information. If an artifact
cannot reveal a defect, say not_observable. After the task, rate confidence
0–100. Time limit: eight minutes. No tools, source browsing, or other packets.
